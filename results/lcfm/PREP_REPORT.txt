1. STOP CONDITION S1 WAS ARGUABLY TRIGGERED AND I CONTINUED. Part 1 found that the
   old-engine seed baseline was contaminated by the `seeds:` config bug, and the
   correction moved a headline number by more than 10% in RELATIVE terms: the 1M
   acceptance difference went from -10.4% to -0.1% (the absolute move is 10.3 points
   of acceptance). The instruction was: finish Part 1, report, and continue only if
   the headline direction holds. It does hold, and it strengthens: the candidate
   engine's 1M acceptance is UNCHANGED rather than down, the 1M speedup rises from
   4.03x to 4.375x, and no other pool was contaminated (audited row by row, section
   10.3.1). I therefore continued. If you disagree with that call, Parts 2-6 rest on
   it and should be re-read with that in mind.

2. THE KIT'S vLLM CONCLUSION WAS WRONG, IN OUR FAVOUR AND AGAINST US. The kit
   declared a vLLM decode-per-token figure uncomputable and fell back to an
   end-to-end comparison in which RASD overtakes vLLM at 512k and 1M. The cause was
   reading results/mlsys/vllm_run/ (whose ttft_s is an absolute monotonic clock
   reading, 1.79e9 s) instead of the corrected rerun results/mlsys/vllm_run2/, which
   recorded prefill and decode separately. With the right file there IS a decode
   comparison, and it is unflattering: our prefill beats vLLM's entire run by
   1.92x / 2.52x / 2.56x, and vLLM's decode is still 130-162x faster per token than
   the candidate engine (429-731x before the fix). Both now appear, in the kit, in
   RESULTS_OCT 10.5 and in the manuscript.

3. RESULTS_OCT 10.2a WAS ALSO WRONG, IN A SMALLER WAY. It claimed the 512k row was
   already like-for-like in its book sets. It is not: the old pool carries five books
   (the n=5 extension added two) against the candidate's three. Matched, 512k spec is
   3.77x against 3.75x pooled — immaterial, but the claim was false and is corrected.
   All six matched cells are now collected in RESULTS_OCT 10.2b, which is the table
   the camera-ready carries.

4. THE DECODE-DIAGNOSIS SETUP HAD TWO LATENT DEFECTS, AND PART 2 HIT BOTH.
   * The STEP c gate demanded `acceptance in (0,1]` and `spec_steps >= 1`, which is
     right for the spec arms it was written for and wrong for a target-only arm. It
     failed a perfectly good 128k target-only gate row and stopped the top-up session
     after $9.06 with one good row and none of the five rows the session existed to
     produce. Fixed in 4db9593: the check is arm-aware and now also verifies the
     engine-provenance columns that stop condition S2 requires, with two regression
     tests that run the real script.
   * The second attempt ran all six rows cleanly ($28.32, 1.27 h).

5. THE TOP-UP LOWERED A RATIO RATHER THAN RAISING IT. The 512k and 1M target-only
   arms were n=1 and n=2 books. With the missing books measured, the spec-to-target
   ratio falls at two of three rungs (2.50x -> 2.29x at 512k, 2.25x -> 2.20x at 1M)
   because the single-book 512k denominator was the slowest book. The thin
   denominator was flattering; the manuscript now quotes the three- and five-book
   figures. The matched old-against-new speedups did not move.

6. FIGURE C CANNOT BE MADE TO AGREE WITH THE TEXT WITHOUT MORE COMPUTE. The text
   quotes the campaign's long-context greedy census (9 of 15 rows loop), whose per-row
   sidecars are NOT in the release. The released per-row verdicts are a different
   campaign at 128k: 9 of 10 loop in the first-window audit and 1 of 10 in the later
   temperature arms. The figure plots what exists and the caption says so; forcing
   agreement would mean recovering the October 1M sidecars from the pod. Flagged as
   TODO AMAN.

7. THE TOOLCHAIN IS NOT WHAT THE BRIEF ASSUMED. latexmk and pdflatex are not installed
   on this machine; the manuscript was compiled with tectonic (Homebrew), which writes
   the same PDF and runs its own passes, and the two checks the brief names are both
   satisfied: zero undefined references or citations, and no overfull box above 5pt
   (the pre-existing 23.4pt overfull table is fixed; the two survivors are 4.2pt and
   0.7pt).
