LCFM 2026 camera-ready: summary of changes

The reviewed version reported that the speedup regresses to parity at 512k and 1M, and
that ring communication was at most 1.2 percent of wall time. Both are withdrawn. A
bare-process benchmark of the ring primitive shows each rotation round costs a fixed
10.3 ms regardless of payload, so the ring was paying per-round latency rather than
bandwidth: removing the rotation is worth 2.8 to 5.3 times on decode, and speculation
still pays 2.0 to 2.3 times on top. Two further claims are corrected: the draft was
conditioned on the prompt's first 4096 tokens rather than on the recent window, and two
long-context seed stages had re-run seed 42 because a level's seed list is overwritten
during config merge, so the corrected 1M acceptance is unchanged rather than down 10
percent. The title now names the three scientific findings. The author block is restored
for the de-anonymized camera-ready: it lists the sole author of the reviewed submission,
with no name added or removed, and its affiliation line reads Independent Researcher.
