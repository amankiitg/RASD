LCFM 2026 camera-ready: summary of changes

The reviewed version reported that the speedup regresses to parity at 512k and 1M, and
that ring communication was at most 1.2 percent of wall time. Both are withdrawn. Every
campaign run used the NCCL blocking wait, which polls each collective wait with a fixed
10 ms host sleep; that is why a rotation round cost the same 10.3 ms at 4 KB and 266 MB,
against 0.134 ms without the flag. What the rotation-free decode removes is two different
costs: at 128k the poll is about 70 percent of it, while at 1M the poll is about 3 percent
and the remainder is not attributed here. Removing the rotation is still worth 2.8 to 4.4
times on decode, with speculation paying 1.5 to 2.3 times on top. Two further corrections:
two long-context seed stages re-ran seed 42 (a level's seed list is overwritten during
config merge), so the corrected 1M acceptance shows no detectable change rather than a 10
percent drop; and the rotary finding's perplexity side is one passage. The author block is
unchanged, affiliated Independent Researcher.
