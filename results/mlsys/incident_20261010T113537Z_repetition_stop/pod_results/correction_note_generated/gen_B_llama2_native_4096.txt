3. IT HAS BEEN CONCEDED BY THE SUPREME COURT OF THE UNITED STATES.
In the case of _Prigg_ _vs._ _Pennsylvania_, decided by the Supreme
Court of the United States, in 1842, Chief Justice Taney, in delivering
the opinion of the court, said: "The power of Congress, _exclusive of
the states_, to legislate upon this subject (slavery) is _well settled_
by numerous decisions of this court."
