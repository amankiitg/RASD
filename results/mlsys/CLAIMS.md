## Must NOT claim

| # | forbidden claim | why |
|---|---|---|
| N1 | Any **1M acceptance mean over documents** beyond n=5, or any distributional statement about how often a model loops. | 5 usable books, 1 seed, 1 temperature; one book (1726) was excluded as a data defect. |
| N2 | "Acceptance is flat or improves with context" as a property of the method. | Greedy (B) showed a **decline** (0.3273→0.2705→0.2132, n=1); sampling (A) shows a rise. Both are n≤5 and A's cross-rung windows share only 91-100% of their scored tokens. C3 states the measured shape, it does not generalize it. |
| N3 | "The model **benefits from** long context", or any quality *improvement* at 1M. | The one exactly-aligned book is **worse** at 1M (+9.5% ppl); the other two differ by ≤1.1% on non-identical windows. Coherence is the claim (C4), benefit is not. |
| N4 | Any statement that the **RASD-vs-vLLM ratio measures the value of speculative decoding**, or any production-serving claim. The vLLM column is a bound on our own target-only path, nothing more. | vLLM 0.6.3 here is **target-only** (no speculative decoding is installed in that venv), runs **bf16 weights and bf16 KV against our FP4/NF4**, and at these contexts **94.2-99.7% of its wall is prefill** (RASD's 1M wall is 86% decode), so the end-to-end ratio compares a prefill-bound wall with a decode-bound one. Our own target-only path is itself ~28x above its floor (C6/C8). |
| N13 | Any attribution of the **429-731x per-decode-token gap** to one cause, or any suggestion that it is a like-for-like engine comparison. | The gap spans fused CUDA kernels + TP=8 + bf16 against a Python ring loop + NF4; the same sidecar cannot separate dequantization, Python overhead and launch cost (N9), and no profiler run exists on the vLLM side. |
| N14 | Any claim of **seed variation**, or any "spread across seeds", or any presentation of the seed stages as a failed replication rather than as a void experiment. | Both seed stages recorded `seed=42` and reproduced the seed-42 token streams **byte-identically** across two sessions and two hosts (C15): the level's `seeds:` key never reaches the sampler. The replicate does not exist, so there is no seed spread to report at any context, and "the seeds agree" is not a finding either. |
| N5 | That sampled decoding is **lossless in the bit-exact sense**. | At T>0 the engine uses `min(1, p_target/p_draft)` rejection with a residual resample; equivalence is distributional, and a sampled row cannot be compared to target-only by token identity. |
| N6 | Any **128k speedup for condition B**. | The 128k target-only arm was lost to the Mac-side SSH drop; B has no 128k denominator. |
| N7 | Any **perplexity** number from the earlier quality rows. | Those scored `ppl_tokens` = 8; the redo is 1024 (C4) and supersedes them. |
| N8 | That the **ring is the dominant cause** of the 42-59x gap, or any **per-rank** attribution of time to communication, or any reading of the 8-rank profiled row as a throughput measurement. | Profile (a) shows 0% communication and a large local cost; the 8-rank row (C8) shows 1.8% communication and 87.9% compute, so both rank counts agree the ring is not the dominant cause. The dedicated 2-rank profile (b) remains **skipped** — no 2-GPU A100 SXM type exists and the only 2-GPU A100 (PCIe) has no capacity — and the 24-token profiled row is not a throughput measurement. |
| N9 | A breakdown of the **local** cost into dequantization vs Python vs kernel launches. | The sidecar's three categories cannot do it, and the change needed to attribute by operator is an engine change, forbidden here because it would invalidate every timing quoted. |
| N10 | "1.658 s at 1 rank is the local share of 3.526 s at 8 ranks." | The 1-rank run holds the whole 128k KV on one rank, so it is not a like-for-like per-rank baseline. Only the weaker statement in C7 holds. |
| N11 | That all six configured 1M books ran. | `pg19_train_1726` failed twice on a 765-token continuation against a 1024-token requirement, and is reported as an exclusion. |
| N12 | That the rope acceptance drop is **explained by a perplexity cost**. | The gate's paired ppl_ratio is 1.0005 (f16) and 1.0156 (f32) — the intervention is nearly free *in likelihood* on the gate's continuation, while acceptance falls 29%. That tension is an open observation to investigate, not something to narrate away. Nor may the drop be claimed for any context other than 128k, any factor above 32, or any n above 3. |
| N15 | That any of the 128k ablation settings is the **optimal** one, or that a 128k optimum **transfers** to 512k or 1M. | `spec_steps` 6 is the edge of the tested range (2 and 4 are below it) and the trend is still rising there; `draft_window_cap` 16384 and T=1.0 are single points at which the other knobs sit at their headline values; and temperature and draft length interact (higher acceptance rewards longer drafts), so the optima are of a 128k grid one setting deep, not of the configuration. The 1M row that matters is 8x longer per step, where the same trade cannot be assumed. |
